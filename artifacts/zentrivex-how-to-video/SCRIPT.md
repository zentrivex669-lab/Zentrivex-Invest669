# Zentrivex — How to Use

## Director's treatment

- **Promise:** Show the real account journey from registration through a withdrawal request, without implying that the demo account has funds or an active investment.
- **Identity:** Use the site's midnight-teal background, gold accent, pale text, and Inter. Use the plain Zentrivex wordmark; no separate logo asset is present in the site.
- **Hero:** A front-facing, iPhone 17 Pro Max-style phone frame containing mobile screenshots captured from the development website and the created demo account.
- **Motion:** Screen-led push-ins, a KYC scan line, and a gold edge that travels between screens. Use two quick compound cuts, then slow the movement for tracking and withdrawal.
- **Arc:** Open with the phone and the destination; move through account setup, verification, funding, plan selection, tracking, and withdrawal; finish on a quiet brand lockup.
- **Payoff:** The six step numerals collapse into one gold ring around the Zentrivex wordmark. The ring opens into the phone edge at the first frame for a clean loop.
- **Format and runtime:** 4:5 portrait, 30.0 seconds, silent captions plus one instrumental bed, no voiceover.
- **Privacy and accuracy:** Do not show an email address, phone number, password, payment destination, or submitted transaction. The demo account has no funds and no investments. Do not imply a deposit, plan, or withdrawal was completed. Do not show plan-return percentages or sample market statistics.

### Shot 01 / 0.00–3.30s / 3.30s — “YOUR ACCOUNT, STEP BY STEP”

**Choreography:** 0.00–0.25s: the opening ring is already visible around the screen edge. 0.25–0.65s: three separate gold edge segments snap into the phone outline, 0.45s apart. 0.70–1.10s: the phone scales from 0.82 to 1.04 and settles to 1.00. 0.95–1.50s: the wordmark unmasks vertically. 1.50–2.15s: the subtitle resolves. 2.15–2.65s: the phone screen pushes in slightly while a gold highlight travels from the registration title to the first field. 2.65–3.30s: the device pushes through the lens; the gold field underline from Shot 02 appears before the cut completes.

**Assets and technique:** Actual blank mobile registration screenshot `public/images/site-register.png`; CSS phone frame and Dynamic Island; gold SVG outline; masked type. No generated imagery.

**Sound intent:** Instrumental starts with a restrained low pulse; a small accent is intended on each bezel snap. Timing is unverified until the track is generated.
